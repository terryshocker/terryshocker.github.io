About the Artist

Real stories. Real sound.

Terry Shocker, born Onome Samson, is a Nigerian recording artist from Warri, Delta State. He began his musical journey in 2020 and creates Afrobeats and Afro-pop music. His music draws inspiration from real-life experiences, ambition, struggle and the everyday realities of life. Through his songs, Terry Shocker aims to connect with listeners through authentic stories and relatable sounds.

Terry Shocker has released numerous songs, collaborations and freestyle projects across digital music platforms. His project No Love for Street continues his focus on real-life experiences and the challenges faced by people working to build better lives.
